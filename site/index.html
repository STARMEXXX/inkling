What Inkling does

The whole extension is about 100 KB of plain JavaScript that you can read yourself.

🖍️

Underlines mistakes as you type

Spelling, grammar, punctuation and wording problems get a colored underline. Click the suggestion to apply it.

💧

Inky, the little helper

A small ink drop sits in the corner of the field you’re typing in. Click it to see every suggestion and fix them all at once.

✍️

Rewrites with AI

Select some text and Inkling can make it clearer, shorter, more formal, friendlier or more confident.

🔒

Keeps your text private

Inkling has no server and collects no analytics. API keys are stored only in your browser.

🌍

Works on most sites

It handles plain text boxes as well as rich editors like the ones in Gmail, Notion and LinkedIn.

🆓

Free to use

Grammar checks cost nothing. Rewrites use your own Claude or OpenAI key, and a typical one costs less than a cent.
